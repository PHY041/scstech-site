01 / Company

Who we are

SCS Technology (Tianjin) Co., Ltd. was founded on 2 August 2022 and is headquartered in Tianjin, China, with production in northern China.

We are an industry-and-trade company. We supply manure belts — PP, PVC and rubber, for in-cage, lateral and inclined sections — egg collection belts and accessories, make our own ultrasonic belt welder, and export complete equipment for poultry cage systems.
