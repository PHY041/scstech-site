Choosing a belt

Which material for which section?

Inside the cage, PP is common. Outside, on lateral and inclined sections, endless rubber belts are common, and PVC is also used. Your system drawing decides.

Is there a standard width?

No. Width follows the cage. Send the drawing or the measured clear width of the cage floor.

Can I order by measuring my old belt?

Not reliably. Belts stretch in use and narrow as they stretch; edges also wear. Use the original order, contract or cage drawing — or measure the cage floor.

Is “PP” a guarantee of quality?

No. “PP” names the main polymer only; fillers and additives vary. Ask for the material to be stated on the quotation. China’s 2023 MARA guidance recommends virgin polypropylene.

Quoting & ordering

Do you publish prices?

We quote by specification. Price depends on material, thickness, width, quantity, joint and delivery terms, so we quote once we know your cage.

Per square metre, per metre or per roll — how do I compare quotes?

Convert to the same unit. Price per linear metre ÷ belt width (m) = price per m². Price per roll ÷ (roll length × width) = price per m². Then check thickness, material, joint and freight are the same.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing. Send a photo of your cage — the drawing too, if you have one. We work out the sizes and confirm them with you before production.

What is the lead time and minimum order?

Confirmed on your quotation for the specification you need. Plan back from your bird-placement date: order confirmation, production, shipping, 12 hours of acclimatisation and installation.

Do you supply OEM / private label?

Ask us with your specification and quantity.

Installation & welding

Are belts welded at the factory or on site?

In-cage belts are usually threaded through the cage and welded into a loop on site. Closed-loop PVC and rubber belts can be delivered as loops. Agree this before ordering.

Can I weld in winter?

Only in a suitable ambient temperature — the T/CAAA 152—2025 draft says ≥10 °C and no welding below 0 °C. Let new belt acclimatise for 12 hours first.

How tight should the belt be?

Only until it stops slipping on the drive roller. Over-tension stretches the belt, damages joints and stops it from tracking.

Tracking & maintenance

Why does my belt run to one side?

Common causes: a roller with manure build-up on one side, a seized or misaligned roller, an uneven frame, uneven tension or a skewed joint. Lock out power, clean and check rollers, then adjust the tensioner at the idler end.

Which side do I tighten?

At the idler (tension) end, tighten the side the belt has run towards, a little at a time, then run and watch from outside the guard.

How long will a belt last?

No honest single figure exists — published claims vary widely and rarely state test conditions. Material, tension, roller condition and cleaning decide it.

Egg belts

What are egg belts made of?

Polypropylene. MARA 2023 specifies high-toughness virgin PP of grade PP5 or above.

How are egg belts joined?

Usually sewn, with the ends overlapping at least 10 cm and all edges finished (T/CAAA 152—2025 draft).

After-sales

What does the warranty cover?

Warranty term and scope are set out on your quotation or contract. Ask us to define what counts as a product defect versus installation or operating causes.

Do you provide installation guidance?

Ask us when you order and we’ll agree the form of support in writing.

My belt has a problem — what should I send?

Purchase date, cage model, tiers, row length, belt material and thickness, and photos or a short video of the problem (taken with the system stopped).
