Are accessories interchangeable between cage brands?

Usually not directly — rollers and tensioners must match the belt width and cage frame. Never fit a smaller roller: tight bends crack joints.

What should I check after each flock?

Scraper contact, roller lagging, idler rotation and tensioner travel; lubricate motors, bearings and gears.
