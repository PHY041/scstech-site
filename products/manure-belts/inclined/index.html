Manure belts · Inclined

Inclined manure belt

The last section of the line: from the lateral belt, out of the house.

Ask about this

01 / Site

Set by your site

  1. 01

    Angle

    Angle = arctan (height difference ÷ horizontal run). A longer run means a gentler angle; a steeper angle makes manure more likely to slide back.

  2. 02

    Width

    Must accept everything the lateral belt delivers.

  3. 03

    Sidewalls / surface

    Depend on whether manure falls off the sides and how wet it is.

  4. 04

    Discharge

    Truck, the farm’s manure line or another destination — tell us where the manure goes.

Where it sits

The last section of the line

The inclined belt takes everything the lateral belt delivers and carries it out of the house to a truck or the farm’s manure line.

02 / Troubleshooting

If manure isn’t moving, check this first

SymptomCheck
Drive roller turns but belt stallsSlipping: check tension, worn lagging, ice on rollers, excess manure
Belt moves but manure slides backCheck angle, surface, sidewalls and manure moisture (fix drinker leaks)

Always lock out before inspecting.

03 / We confirm

What we confirm with you

  1. Lateral belt width
  2. Height difference and horizontal run
  3. Discharge point (truck, manure line, other)
  4. Manure dry or wet
  5. Winter minimum
  6. Site photos

What incline angle is right?

There is no universal angle. It is calculated from the height to lift and the horizontal run available.

Do I need sidewalls?

Only if manure falls off the sides. Sidewalls are not the same as guide strips, which are for tracking.
