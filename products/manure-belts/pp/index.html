Main product

PP manure belt

Our main product. White polypropylene manure belt for any poultry cage system, under every tier. Supplied in rolls, sized to your cage — send a photo and we confirm the size with you.

Ask about this

01 / Position

Where it goes

One belt per tier, running the full length of the row beneath the cage floor. At the drive end, a scraper drops the manure onto the lateral belt. A closed loop: the upper run carries manure, the lower run returns.

PP belt

Lighter than water. Resistant to the acids and alkalis in manure.

02 / Material

Why polypropylene

  • Light — PP density is about 0.90–0.91 g/cm³, lighter than water.
  • Resistant to the non-oxidising acids and alkalis in manure.
  • Recommended material: China’s 2023 MARA technical guidance for stacked layer housing states manure conveyor belts should preferably use virgin polypropylene with anti-static, anti-ageing and anti-deviation properties.

Note

Know the limits

PP becomes more brittle at low temperatures and slowly stretches under constant tension, more so in summer heat. Formulation, correct tension and a square installation matter more than the material name. If your house runs cold in winter, tell us the minimum temperature when you enquire.

04 / Installation

Installation notes

  1. Let new belt acclimatise in the house for 12 hours before installing.
  2. Weld joints in a suitable ambient temperature: the T/CAAA 152—2025 draft calls for ≥10 °C and no welding below 0 °C.
  3. Tension evenly on both sides — only until the belt stops slipping; over-tension stretches the belt and stops it tracking.
  4. Run empty before birds go in.
  5. Lock out power before adjusting tension, cleaning rollers or making a joint.

What thickness of PP manure belt is typical?

For stacked layer cages, 1.0 mm and 1.2 mm are the most common. Choose based on row length, manure moisture and installation accuracy; we confirm thickness on your quotation.

How long does a PP manure belt last?

There is no reliable single figure. Life depends on the material, tension, roller condition, manure build-up on rollers and whether the belt edge rubs the frame.

Can PP belts be joined by high-frequency welding?

Generally not — PP is non-polar. PP belts are joined by ultrasonic or hot-plate welding. PVC belts can be RF-welded.

Do PP belts crack in winter?

PP becomes more brittle in the cold; how cold depends on the formulation. Tell us your minimum house temperature and avoid welding below 0 °C.
