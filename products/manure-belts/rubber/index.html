Manure belts · Rubber

Rubber manure belt

Endless black rubber belts for the sections outside the cage.

Quote this

01 / Position

Where rubber belts work

Inside the cage, PP is the usual choice. Outside it — on the lateral section at the row end and the inclined section leaving the house — endless rubber belts are common. Rubber and PP divide the work by position; one does not replace the other.

02 / Specify

What to tell us

  1. Lateral or inclined
  2. Width and loop length
  3. Cage model, tiers, row length (or drawing)
  4. Where the manure goes
  5. Minimum winter temperature

Is a rubber manure belt just a different colour of PP belt?

No. The materials differ, and so do the typical positions, thickness, joint method and cold behaviour. Specify each separately.

Can rubber manure belts be used in very cold regions?

Check the cold rating of the specific belt in writing and tell us your site’s winter minimum.
