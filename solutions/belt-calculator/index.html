How many metres of manure belt do I need?

Estimate your belt quantity in a minute. We’ll check it against your drawing before you order.

Quantity (m²) ≈ belt width × (row length × 2 + roller allowance) × tiers × rows

Inputs

Where the numbers come from

  1. 01

    Width

    Cage drawing (no universal width).

  2. 02

    Row length

    Tape or drawing; if rows differ, calculate each.

  3. 03

    Tiers & rows

    Count the tiers; take the number of rows from the drawing.

  4. 04

    Allowance

    Wrap around drive and idler rollers plus take-up travel (the T/CAAA 152—2025 draft requires at least 400 mm of tension adjustment at the idler).

Rough sizing

Only know the flock size?

You can only estimate the order of magnitude. At 450–540 cm² of cage floor per hen (MARA), 100,000 hens need 4,500–5,400 m² of cage floor; with the return run, belt quantity is roughly 9,000–10,800 m². Order from the drawing, not from this estimate.

Note

Lateral and inclined belts

The calculator covers in-cage belts only. Lateral and inclined belts are sized from the end-of-house layout.

Why multiply the row length by two?

A manure belt is a closed loop: the upper run carries manure and the lower run returns, each the length of the row.

Is belt area just row length × width?

No — that is only the upper run of one tier. Use width × (2 × row length + allowance) × tiers × rows.

Should I add an allowance?

Yes. The belt wraps around both end rollers and needs take-up travel. Take the figure from the cage drawing.
