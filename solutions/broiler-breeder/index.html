Solution · Broiler & breeder

Belts for broiler and breeder cages

Different cage, same principle: the belt is built to the cage.

Ask about this

01 / Approach

Built from your cage maker’s drawing

SCS supplies parts for cage systems for layers, broilers and breeders. Broiler and breeder cages vary widely in floor width, tier count and row length, so we work from your cage maker’s drawing rather than a catalogue size.

The same rules apply: width follows the cage floor, a closed-loop belt is about twice the row length plus roller wrap and take-up, and the joint method must suit the material.

Are broiler cage manure belts the same as layer cage belts?

The principle is the same but dimensions differ by cage. We specify from your drawing.

Can you supply a belt for another manufacturer’s cage?

Fit depends on dimensions and rollers, not the cage brand. Send the drawing plus drive and idler roller diameters, and say whether the old belt had guide strips.
